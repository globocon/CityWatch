# CityWatch Live Patrol Map — What It Can Do

## See everything, live
- One map shows every patrol car and guard on duty, moving in real time.
- An **online counter** always shows how many cars and guards are active right now — tap it for the full list, and tap any name to jump straight to them on the map.
- The map is honest about freshness: you can see at a glance if someone is moving, stopped, or has gone quiet.

## Check on anyone in seconds
- Search by guard name, car callsign or site.
- Tap any car or guard to see who they are, their street address right now, speed and direction, which site they are at, and their phone battery level.
- **Follow** locks the map onto a vehicle as it drives.
- **Track Live** asks a phone to report every few seconds for 15 minutes — for when you need to watch closely.
- **Ping** asks a phone for its position immediately.

## Message the field straight from the map
- Send a short message from the control room to any one phone — or to **every online car or guard at once**.
- It appears as a notification on their phone whether the app is open or not.

## Proof of patrol
- Every patrol is recorded automatically. **Replay any shift** and watch the exact route as it was driven, coloured by time of day, with every stop and every site visit marked.
- Checkpoint scans confirm arrivals and departures at client sites.
- For patrol cars, the planned run is shown next to what was actually driven.

## Automatic alerts
- A duress alarm appears on the map instantly, with the nearest units shown so you can respond fast.
- The control room is alerted when a unit arrives at a site, sits too long in one place, or stops reporting.

## Simple to run
- Works in any web browser — desktop, tablet or phone. Nothing to install in the control room.
- Standard, satellite and night-shift (dark) map styles.
- Finished shifts clear away automatically, so the map always shows only who is really out there.
